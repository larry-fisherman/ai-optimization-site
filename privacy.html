Analytics

When analytics is enabled, we use Plausible Analytics to count visits. Plausible doesn’t use cookies and doesn’t store personal data. We only see aggregate statistics, such as how many people visited a page. Plausible describes what it collects in its data policy.

Besides page views, we count these events: CTA Click (a click on a call-to-action button), Email Fallback Click (a click on the email link under the contact form), Contact Form Submit (a message sent successfully), Contact Form Error (a message that failed to send) and 404 (a visit to a page that doesn’t exist). Click events record the text of the link you clicked (label) and the address of the page you were on (page). The 404 event records the address that was requested (path).
