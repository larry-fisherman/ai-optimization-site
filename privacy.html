Privacy notice

What this site collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

This site is run by Legible Technologies.

Privacy questions and requests: contact@legibletechnologies.com

Effective date: 14 October 2026

What we collect

We collect only what you send us: your email address, your website address and your message if you use the contact form, and your email address, your message and anything else your email includes if you email us. Our web host also records standard server logs, described under Hosting.

Analytics

This site does not use analytics, and sets no cookies.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, we collect your website address, your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you.

The form is processed by Formspree (privacy policy: https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy). They receive the fields you submit plus technical data such as your IP address and browser details, and pass your message on to us. We keep your message for up to 24 months, then delete it, or earlier on request.

Email

If you email contact@legibletechnologies.com, including through the email link under the contact form, we receive your email address, your message and anything else your email includes. We use it to reply to you and may contact you about your enquiry. If we send you a commercial email, it will include a way to opt out. We keep emails for up to 24 months, then delete them, or earlier on request.

Cookies and advertising

This site sets no cookies and shows no advertising, and it does no advertising or cross-context tracking. Our fonts are hosted on this site rather than loaded from a font service.

If you send the contact form without JavaScript, or if the page’s scripts didn’t load, your browser may go to Formspree’s own page. Their pages and policies, including on cookies, may differ from ours.

Hosting

This site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any website, our web host processes your IP address and request details (such as the page requested and your browser type) in standard server logs, to serve the site and protect it from abuse. Cloudflare sets how long it keeps those logs.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing contact@legibletechnologies.com.

We do not sell or share personal information. If you live in a US state with a privacy law, you may have the right to access, correct and delete your personal information, to the extent that law applies. Email us to make a request and we will reply within 45 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the effective date above.
