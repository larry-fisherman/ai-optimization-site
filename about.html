Who is Legible.

A studio that makes websites readable by AI assistants and answer engines, so businesses get found, understood and represented accurately.

What Legible does.

Legible Technologies is a company that helps businesses improve their AI visibility in an ever-changing environment. AI assistants and answer engines keep changing how they find and describe businesses, so we help keep your site readable to them and your business described accurately.

How we measure it.

We ask a fixed set of questions, the same way each time, across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. Then we track what comes back.

Mention rate
The share of answers that name you.
Citation rate
The share of answers that link to or cite your site.
AI referral traffic
Visits that come from AI assistants, from your own analytics.
Accuracy
Whether what they say about you is correct.
