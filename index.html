[01 / THE SHIFT]

Search used to return links. Now it returns answers.

When someone asks an assistant who to hire, where to go or what to buy, it doesn’t hand them a page of links. It reads the web, decides what it believes, and replies in a few sentences.

If it can’t make sense of your site, it doesn’t say so. It paraphrases a competitor whose site it could read.

Reader 01A person sees

  • A beautiful product photo
  • A friendly headline
  • A button that says “Learn more”

Reader 02A model sees

  • img — no alt, ignored
  • h1 — no entity, no claim
  • a — “learn more” about what?

Legible makes sure both readers leave with the same, correct picture of who you are and what you do.

# Legible

> Legible is a studio that makes websites readable by AI assistants and answer engines, so businesses get found, understood and accurately cited by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI search.

Every website now has two readers: a person with a browser, and a language model that reads the page, decides what it believes and answers in a few sentences. Legible makes sure both come away with the same, correct picture. We work with businesses of any kind and size.

## Services

- AI Visibility Audit: how a business shows up across AI assistants today, and who gets cited instead.
- Machine-Readable Foundations: schema and JSON-LD, llms.txt, semantic structure and crawl access.
- Answer-Ready Content: restructuring pages into the clear, citable claims that language models retrieve.
- Brand Signal Monitoring: tracking how AI describes a business over time, and fixing drift at the source.
- Internal AI Readiness: helping teams adopt AI tools well (optional add-on).

## Method

1. Read: ask the assistants customers use, and map what they say and who they cite.
2. Restructure: rebuild key pages around clear entities and citable claims.
3. Signal: make the facts that matter consistent everywhere AI looks.
4. Monitor: keep asking, and correct the source when answers drift.
